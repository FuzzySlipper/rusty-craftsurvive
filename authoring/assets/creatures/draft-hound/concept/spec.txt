THIS DESIGN - THE DRAFT-HOUND. A lean pack hunter of the veil forest, the windy chambers where the cave's draft blows: about 1.1 m at the shoulder and 2.2 m long, NOT a dog: its head is a bat's. A long-legged, narrow, deep-chested body with knobbly joints, a short bare tail, and the head of a giant leaf-nosed bat: a short muzzle with a wrinkled, upturned leaf-shaped nose, a wide mouth of long thin fangs, and tiny sunken pale eyes. The skin is nearly hairless, wrinkled and pale bone-grey, with a short sparse straw-coloured coat only along the spine and shoulders, and darker bark-brown down the legs. THE KEY FEATURE: two enormous, thin, translucent ears like the forest's lace fans, wide and rounded, larger than its head, veined and lace-edged, which it holds square into the draft to smell and hear what comes on it. A pack stands all facing the same way, into the wind.

IT MUST NOT READ CUTE: no smile, no big round eyes, no soft baby proportions, no pet-like face. Unsettling and wild, like a real predator seen up close.
