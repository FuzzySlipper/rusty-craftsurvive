SHAPE - a real naked mole-rat's anatomy simplified into a few large masses: a long, low, sausage-shaped body in loose heavy folds of skin, short strong legs with long pale digging claws, a blunt wedge-shaped head as wide as the neck, and a short thin naked tail. NO ears on the outside, only tiny holes.

THE FACE, THE MOST IMPORTANT PART: unsettling and wild, never cute. Tiny, sunken, near-blind eyes, two small dark slits. The mouth stands OPEN: the lips drawn back hard from FOUR HUGE yellow-orange incisors, two upper and two lower, each as long as the head is deep, thick and solid, the upper pair curving down and the lower pair curving up, sticking well out in front of the lips. Behind them the mouth is open, a dark gap with the pink inside showing. IN THE FRONT VIEW TOO: the four incisors stand out plainly, the blunt snout above them; never a smile, never round cheeks, never big eyes.

PAINT - follow the SECOND reference image, a sheet of a giant ground sloth from the same game, only for its treatment and not its colours: a hand-painted texture at LOW RESOLUTION with crisp visible square texels, a few authored pixel-art marks and dark line work. Loose wrinkled skin of pale pinkish grey, with dark line work in the folds; on the back and upper flanks, crusted patches of cream and rust-orange bacterial mat, as in the first reference image. The incisors yellow-orange. Calm surfaces with a few strong authored details, never a busy tiled pattern, and never a patchwork of flat polygon-shaped colour blocks. Flat even lighting: paint the colours of the creature, not a light direction.
