Exactly the same animal in three ORTHOGRAPHIC views, no perspective, flat even lighting with no cast shadows, on a plain flat light-grey background:

- BOTTOM LEFT: the FRONT view, looking straight at its face, both forelegs and the shoulders visible.

Landscape aspect about 1536x1024. Keep the views separate with clear grey space between them; do not overlap them. All three views at the same scale.

SHAPE - follow the first reference image, the concept sheet of this animal: FAITHFUL real proportions, simplified into a few large smooth-shaded masses. very powerful humped shoulders and a deep barrel chest on thick straight legs ending in cloven hooves, a short thick neck far stockier than a giraffe's, and a broad heavy head. KEEP THE BODY, COAT AND STRIPES of the first attached Sivatherium sheet exactly, and change ONLY the head ornaments. THESE ARE NOT ANTLERS: no tines, no prongs, no moose palms, and no shed-able bone. They are OSSICONES, like a giraffe's, covered in the same short skin and coat as the head, with soft rounded blunt ends. The rear pair are two broad, thick, short, flattened paddle-shaped lobes that rise from the back of the skull and spread sideways like a pair of stubby oars, each with at most one shallow notch in its edge, no longer than the head itself. A second smaller pair are simple short rounded cones directly above the eyes. A long tongue-ready mobile muzzle like an okapi's. Large ears set low. A thin tufted tail. Short glossy coat. Colour: deep plum-brown body shading to near-black on the shoulders, with OKAPI-LIKE sharp cream horizontal stripes across the haunches and upper legs, pale stockings, a pale face with a dark brow. Bull-like and stately. NOT a moose, NOT a deer, NOT a cow, NOT a giraffe.

PAINT - follow the second reference image, the sheet of a giant ground sloth from the same game, only for its treatment and not its colours: a hand-painted texture at LOW RESOLUTION with crisp visible square texels (about 32 texels per metre, nearest-neighbour), a few authored pixel-art marks and dark line work. Calm surfaces with a few strong authored details, never a busy tiled pattern, and never a patchwork of flat polygon-shaped colour blocks. Flat even lighting: paint the colours of the animal, not a light direction.

PROHIBITIONS: NO text, NO letters, NO labels, NO UI, NO watermark, NO frame lines. NO people, NO scale post, NO rocks, NO water, NO other objects. NOT photoreal, NOT realistic fur strands, NOT a perspective render, NOT anime, NOT cel shading or outlines.
