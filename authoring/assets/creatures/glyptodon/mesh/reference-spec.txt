SHAPE - follow the first reference image, the concept sheet of this animal: FAITHFUL real proportions, simplified into a few large smooth-shaded masses. The shell is one long, seamless, elongated loaf or bell shape, much longer than it is tall, highest toward the REAR and sloping down to the front, and its sides fall almost straight down nearly to the ground, hiding most of the legs. There is NO overhanging rim, NO flared edge, NO belly plate and NO large turtle scutes. The shell surface is made of about 1800 small six-sided bony studs, each a low rounded cone, grouped into many small rosettes with deep grooves between: it reads as a fine knobbly honeycomb, like a giant pineapple skin or a cobbled dome, with double rows of larger studs along the lower edge. The head is short, deep and blunt, more like a heavy tapir or a small hippo than a tortoise: a tall skull, very deep jaws, a broad soft muzzle, and on top a smooth convex bony cap of fused plates. The tail is thick and tapering, armoured in eight or nine separate rings of big conical spikes, stacked like a cone of rings, ending in a short tube. The legs are short thick columns with small blunt claws. Colour: dusty warm ochre-grey studs with darker grooves, a paler band along the lower edge, dark greyish skin. Slow, heavy and odd.
