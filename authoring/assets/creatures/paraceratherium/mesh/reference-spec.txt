WHAT IT IS: a model reference sheet for building a 3D game creature: ONE a Paraceratherium, about 7.4 m long and 4.8 m at the shoulder, standing in a NEUTRAL STANCE on all four feet: legs straight down and slightly apart so every leg is clearly visible and separate, mouth closed, head level and looking straight ahead, neck carried forward and level, not raised, short tail down. Exactly the same animal in three ORTHOGRAPHIC views, no perspective, flat even lighting with no cast shadows, on a plain flat light-grey background:
- TOP HALF of the sheet: the LEFT SIDE profile, head pointing to the left, the whole body from nose to tail filling the width.
- BOTTOM LEFT: the FRONT view, looking straight at its face, both forelegs and the shoulders visible.
- BOTTOM RIGHT: the BACK view, from straight behind, the tail toward the viewer and both hind legs visible.
Landscape aspect about 1536x1024. Keep the views separate with clear grey space between them; do not overlap them. All three views at the same scale.

SHAPE - follow the first reference image, the concept sheet of this animal: FAITHFUL real proportions, simplified into a few large smooth-shaded masses. immense height. Very long pillar legs, straight as columns, with long lower limbs and three-toed padded feet: this is a tall animal, not a wide one. A long neck of over two metres, thick at the base, carried forward and low, rising into a hump of long spines along the top of the shoulders. A long, smooth, hornless skull with an arched top profile and NO horn at all. A long mobile prehensile upper lip, drooping a little like a short tapir snout. One pair of downward-pointing conical tusks at the tip of the upper jaw and a shorter pair pointing forward from the lower jaw. Moderately large ears. A short tail. Thick folded hairless skin with deep creases at the shoulder, hip and neck. Colour: pale ash-grey, dusted warm at the belly, darker in the folds. Calm and towering, like a moving tree. NOT an elephant, NOT a giraffe, NOT a normal rhinoceros: it has no horn and no trunk.

PAINT - follow the second reference image, the sheet of a giant ground sloth from the same game, only for its treatment and not its colours: a hand-painted texture at LOW RESOLUTION with crisp visible square texels (about 32 texels per metre, nearest-neighbour), a few authored pixel-art marks and dark line work. Calm surfaces with a few strong authored details, never a busy tiled pattern, and never a patchwork of flat polygon-shaped colour blocks. Flat even lighting: paint the colours of the animal, not a light direction.

PROHIBITIONS: NO text, NO letters, NO labels, NO UI, NO watermark, NO frame lines. NO people, NO scale post, NO rocks, NO water, NO other objects. NOT photoreal, NOT realistic fur strands, NOT a perspective render, NOT anime, NOT cel shading or outlines.
