SHAPE - a real elephant shrew's anatomy simplified into a few large masses: a lean body, long hind legs and short forelegs, a LONG thin flexible snout, big thin naked ears, a long thin naked tail.

THE FACE: wild and wary, NOT cute: small black eyes set high and far back on the head, never big or round or shiny, the snout long and narrow, the mouth a thin straight line, the ears pressed back. Lean and scrawny, never chubby, never a pet.

PAINT - follow the SECOND reference image, a sheet of a giant ground sloth from the same game, only for its treatment and not its colours: a hand-painted texture at LOW RESOLUTION with crisp visible square texels, a few authored pixel-art marks and dark line work. Calm surfaces with a few strong authored details, never a busy tiled pattern, and never a patchwork of flat polygon-shaped colour blocks. Flat even lighting: paint the colours of the creature, not a light direction. Short coarse pale grey-brown fur with darker line work, a wine-red stripe running down the middle of the back from the neck to the tail, the ears, snout tip, feet and tail bare pinkish grey.

PROHIBITIONS: NO text, NO letters, NO labels, NO UI, NO watermark, NO frame lines. NO people, NO scale post, NO water, NO rocks, NO other objects. ONE creature only. NOT cute, NOT a cartoon, NOT photoreal, NOT a perspective render, NOT anime, NOT cel shading or outlines. EXACTLY FOUR LEGS, EXACTLY TWO EYES. NO whiskers. NOT holding anything.
