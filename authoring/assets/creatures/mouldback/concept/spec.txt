THIS DESIGN - THE MOULDBACK. A heavy, blind digging predator of the damp dead ends deep in a cave, where fungi crowd: about 1.4 m at the shoulder and 3 m long, built like a giant badger or mole crossed with a bear: a low powerful body, short thick legs, huge spade-like forepaws with long pale digging claws, and a long snout ending in a ring of pink fleshy feelers like a star-nosed mole's. It has no eyes at all. Its skin is nearly hairless and pale, a cold pinkish grey, wrinkled at the joints. THE KEY FEATURE: a crust of living fungus grows down its back and flanks: clusters of tan and cream mushroom caps, shelf brackets, and a few small caps that glow a faint blue-green, so when it lies still in the damp dark it is just another patch of mushrooms.

STYLE - THE MOST IMPORTANT PART. Match the first attached reference image, a sheet of a giant ground sloth from the same game, only for its treatment and not its shape or colours: a LOW-POLYGON 3D game creature with simple smooth-shaded forms from a few large masses, and a hand-painted texture at LOW RESOLUTION with crisp visible square texels (nearest-neighbour filtering), so hide, markings and eyes read as a few authored pixel-art marks with dark line work - a carefully authored retro texture on a modern-lit model, NOT a PS1 emulation, no jitter or blur. Calm surfaces with a few strong authored details, never a tiled or busy pattern, and never a patchwork of flat polygon-shaped colour blocks.

WHAT IT IS: a model turnaround sheet from a game studio character viewer, for ONE creature. Three views of the SAME creature side by side, all full body, at eye level, on a plain flat warm-grey floor and backdrop: (1) strict side profile walking, one forepaw lifted, (2) three-quarter front view standing, snout feelers spread toward the viewer, (3) threat display: reared up on its hind legs, forepaws raised with the claws out, mouth open showing blunt teeth. Beside view 1 stands a plain dark-grey upright post exactly 1.8 m tall for scale. Landscape aspect about 1536x1024. Equal spacing, nothing overlapping or cropped.

SETTING: an animal of the caves under the world of a painterly early-modern frontier fantasy game whose world looks like Valheim: simple smooth-shaded forms, small textures, atmosphere carrying the mood. Underground most animals are dangerous. It must read as a real, believable animal adapted to the cave, with real anatomy simplified into a few masses: not a monster, not a machine, not a cartoon.

PROHIBITIONS: NO text, NO letters, NO labels, NO UI, NO watermark, NO frame. NO people. NO scenery, rocks or plants on the sheet. EXACTLY FOUR LEGS, each clearly separate. NO eyes. NOT a biomechanical creature, NO shell plates with glowing inset organs. NOT photoreal, NOT realistic fur strands, NOT anime, NOT chibi, NOT cute, NOT toon outlines or cel shading, NOT triangle-faceted polygon art.
