THIS DESIGN - THE WALL RUNNER, a ruin skitterer: a small, flat, quick creature about 1 m long and low to the ground that runs along walls and ceilings. Its BODY, as in the third attached image (its original concept): a low, wide, flattened shell like a smooth pale ivory lozenge with jade-green edges and a thin seam of amber sockets down its back; under it plum-pink ribbed tissue; four long thin limbs in pale casings spread wide to the sides, each ending in a broad round flat suction pad; a long tapering tail-whip of pale segments. No head: the front of the shell is a smooth blunt edge.

RENDERING ANCHOR - THE MOST IMPORTANT PART. The second attached image is a crop from the approved sheet of the main ruin inhabitant, the creature this one must sit beside in the game. Match ITS rendering exactly: the same texel size (small, fine, crisp square texels, about as many across a limb as in that crop - not larger, not chunky blocks), the same hand-painted pixel marks, the same soft modern lighting on smooth low-polygon forms, the same ivory-cream shell surface with soft jade-green edge bands, amber insets set into sockets, pale casings and plum-pink ribbed tissue. NOT a smooth untextured render, NOT faceted, NOT a checkerboard of big blocks. Copy only its rendering and materials, not its body.

DESIGN REFERENCE: the third attached image is this creature's original concept painting. Take its body design from it, NOT its ink-and-wash illustration rendering and not its scenery; render it as the rendering anchor shows.

HOW IT MOVES: it darts low and fast with its limbs spread wide, the tail streaming behind, and clings flat to walls.

IT MUST NOT READ CUTE, and it must NOT read like the xenomorph from the Alien films or H. R. Giger's art: no skull face, no exposed teeth, no glossy black, no tubes and hoses, no wet slime. Strange and unsettling, like something grown rather than built.

STYLE. Match the first attached reference image, a sheet of a giant ground sloth from the same game, only for its treatment and not its shape or colours: a LOW-POLYGON 3D game creature with simple smooth-shaded forms from a few large masses, and a hand-painted texture at LOW RESOLUTION with crisp visible square texels (nearest-neighbour filtering), so shell, tissue, organs and eyes read as a few authored pixel-art marks with dark line work - a carefully authored retro texture on a modern-lit model, NOT a PS1 emulation, no jitter or blur. Calm surfaces with a few strong authored details, never a tiled or busy pattern, and never a patchwork of flat polygon-shaped colour blocks.

WHAT IT IS: a model turnaround sheet from a game studio character viewer, for ONE creature. Three views of the SAME creature side by side, all full body, at eye level, on a plain flat warm-grey floor and backdrop: (1) strict side profile standing low on its four spread limbs, the tail behind, (2) darting, three-quarter front view, limbs splayed wide, (3) threat: the front reared up off the ground, the two front pads raised and spread, the tail whip curled up over its back ready to lash. Beside view 1 stands a plain dark-grey upright post exactly 1.8 m tall for scale. Landscape aspect about 1536x1024. Equal spacing, nothing overlapping or cropped.

SETTING: an inhabitant of the ruins of the ancients, in the caves under the world of a painterly early-modern frontier fantasy game whose world looks like Valheim: simple smooth-shaded forms, small textures, atmosphere carrying the mood.

PROHIBITIONS: NO EYES and NO FACE on the creature. NO text, NO letters, NO labels, NO UI, NO watermark, NO frame. NO people. NO scenery, ruins, rocks or plants on the sheet.
