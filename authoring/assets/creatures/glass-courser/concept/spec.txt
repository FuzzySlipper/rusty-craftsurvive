THIS DESIGN - THE GLASS-COURSER. A sight hunter of the glass steppe's wide open floors, the one cave animal that hunts by eye, because the steppe makes its own light: a lean, long-legged, deep-chested runner about 1.2 m at the shoulder and 3 m long with its tail, built for speed like a cheetah, with a small head, huge dark round eyes and a long thin jaw of small sharp teeth. THE KEY FEATURES: smooth, hairless, milky skin, pale blue-grey and faintly translucent like the steppe's glass, darker slate along the back; and a crest of long, stiff, glassy filaments down its spine and tail, each with a small bead at the tip that glows warm amber like the steppe's feelers, so lying flat in the steppe it is one more glowing tuft.

STYLE - THE MOST IMPORTANT PART. Match the first attached reference image, a sheet of a giant ground sloth from the same game, only for its treatment and not its shape or colours: a LOW-POLYGON 3D game creature with simple smooth-shaded forms from a few large masses, and a hand-painted texture at LOW RESOLUTION with crisp visible square texels (nearest-neighbour filtering), so hide, markings and eyes read as a few authored pixel-art marks with dark line work - a carefully authored retro texture on a modern-lit model, NOT a PS1 emulation, no jitter or blur. Calm surfaces with a few strong authored details, never a tiled or busy pattern, and never a patchwork of flat polygon-shaped colour blocks.

HABITAT - the second attached reference image is a painting of the cave community this animal lives in. Use it ONLY for the animal's colours and materials, so the animal plainly belongs there. Do NOT draw that scenery, its growths or the small human figure, and do NOT copy the painting's rendering style.

WHAT IT IS: a model turnaround sheet from a game studio character viewer, for ONE creature. Three views of the SAME creature side by side, all full body, at eye level, on a plain flat warm-grey floor and backdrop: (1) strict side profile running at full stretch, (2) lying flat on the floor with its glowing crest raised like a tuft, three-quarter view, (3) threat display: standing, head low, jaws open showing the teeth. Beside view 1 stands a plain dark-grey upright post exactly 1.8 m tall for scale. Landscape aspect about 1536x1024. Equal spacing, nothing overlapping or cropped.

SETTING: an animal of the caves under the world of a painterly early-modern frontier fantasy game whose world looks like Valheim: simple smooth-shaded forms, small textures, atmosphere carrying the mood. Underground most animals are dangerous. It must read as a real, believable animal adapted to the cave, with real anatomy simplified into a few masses: not a monster, not a machine, not a cartoon.

PROHIBITIONS: NO text, NO letters, NO labels, NO UI, NO watermark, NO frame. NO people. NO scenery, rocks or plants on the sheet. EXACTLY FOUR LEGS, each clearly separate. NOT a biomechanical creature, NO shell plates with glowing inset organs. NOT photoreal, NOT realistic fur strands, NOT anime, NOT chibi, NOT cute, NOT toon outlines or cel shading, NOT triangle-faceted polygon art.
