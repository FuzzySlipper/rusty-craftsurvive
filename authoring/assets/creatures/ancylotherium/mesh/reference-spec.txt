SHAPE - follow the first reference image, the concept sheet of this animal: FAITHFUL real proportions, simplified into a few large smooth-shaded masses. a body that slopes steeply: the long forelimbs hold the high shoulders up and the short hind limbs drop the hips much lower, so the back runs downhill from front to rear like a giant goat crossed with a gorilla. The head is long and horse-like with a blunt muzzle, and the forehead swells into a smooth rounded dome. Long mobile neck. The forefeet end not in hooves but in large curved hooked claws, three on each forefoot, and it walks flat on the sole of the foot with the claws curled up clear of the ground. The hind feet are short with smaller claws. Short tail. Short close coat with a low dark mane along the neck. Colour: sandy buff with a soft pale belly, dark chocolate legs from the knee down, a dark muzzle and faint dark dappling over the shoulders. Gentle, odd, unmistakably not a horse. NOT a horse, NOT a giraffe, NOT a ground sloth.

Flat even lighting: paint the colours of the animal, not a light direction.
