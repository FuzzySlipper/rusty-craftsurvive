THIS DESIGN - THE CANYON STALKER. Shoulder about 2.6 m high, so the 1.8 m post reaches about to its belly. Long legs held bent and crouched, the elbows and hocks high, body hunched and coiled forward as if about to spring, far heavier in front than behind. A huge blunt wedge of a head on a short massive neck, thrust forward and low, with a jaw that seems to split the head in two, and a ridge of bony plates running from the brow along the neck. Hide the rust red and burnt orange of canyon rock, banded with dark broken stripes across the back and legs, pale bone-coloured teeth and plates. Eyes small, amber and forward-facing. Heavy blunt hooked claws on two toes.
STYLE - THE MOST IMPORTANT PART. Match the second attached reference image, a sheet of a giant ground sloth from the same game, only for its treatment and not its shape or colours: a LOW-POLYGON 3D game creature with simple smooth-shaded forms from a few large masses, and a hand-painted texture at LOW RESOLUTION with crisp visible square texels (nearest-neighbour filtering), so hide, markings and eyes read as a few authored pixel-art marks with dark line work - a carefully authored retro texture on a modern-lit model, NOT a PS1 emulation, no jitter or blur. Calm surfaces with a few strong authored details, never a tiled or busy pattern, and never a patchwork of flat polygon-shaped colour blocks.

STARTING POINT: the first attached reference image is only where this design starts. Take the menace of its long head, its huge gaping jaw of conical teeth, and its high shoulders and hackled crest. THIS IS NOT THAT ANIMAL and not any real animal, living or extinct. It is an invented, unnerving wild predator from the high, dry, empty places of a fantasy frontier. It must read as dangerous from its silhouette alone, from far away.

THE KEY FEATURE: EXCESSIVELY LONG LEGS. The legs are far too long for the body, thin and jointed like stilts, and this wrongness is the point. EXACTLY FOUR LEGS, each clearly separate from the others with clear space between them, never fused. The body is lean, not fat: a narrow deep chest, a tucked waist, tendons and bone visible under tight hide.

WHAT IT IS: a model turnaround sheet from a game studio character viewer, for ONE creature. Three views of the SAME creature side by side, all full body, at eye level, on a plain flat warm-grey floor and backdrop: (1) strict side profile stalking, one foreleg lifted, (2) three-quarter front view standing still, head low and staring at the viewer, (3) threat display: jaws gaping very wide open, showing the dark red mouth interior, tongue and every long tooth, upper and lower jaw clearly separate. Beside view 1 stands a plain dark-grey upright post exactly 1.8 m tall for scale. Landscape aspect about 1536x1024. Equal spacing, nothing overlapping or cropped.

SETTING: a creature for a painterly early-modern frontier fantasy game whose world looks like Valheim: simple smooth-shaded forms, small textures, atmosphere carrying the mood.

PROHIBITIONS: NO text, NO letters, NO labels, NO UI, NO watermark, NO frame. NO people. NO extra legs, NO wings. NOT photoreal, NOT realistic fur strands, NOT anime, NOT chibi, NOT cute, NOT toon outlines or cel shading, NOT triangle-faceted polygon art.
