WHAT IT IS: a model reference sheet for building a 3D game creature: ONE root-adder, a giant constrictor snake of the caves, in exactly the head, scales and colours of the FIRST reference image. About 7 m long from snout to tail tip and as thick as a man's thigh at its middle. For the 3D model it lies PERFECTLY STRAIGHT: the whole body stretched out flat on the ground in ONE STRAIGHT LINE from the head to the tail tip, with NO curves, NO bends, NO coils, NO S-shape, the head resting level on the ground at the front end and looking straight ahead, the body tapering gently to a pointed tail. Exactly the same snake in three ORTHOGRAPHIC views, no perspective, flat even lighting with no cast shadows, on a plain flat light-grey background:
- TOP OF THE SHEET: the LEFT SIDE profile, head pointing to the left, the whole straight snake from snout to tail tip filling the width of the sheet, as a long thin straight band.
- BOTTOM LEFT: the FRONT view, looking straight at its face end-on: the head and the body behind it, small.
- BOTTOM RIGHT: the BACK view, from straight behind the tail tip: the tail and body end-on, small.
Landscape aspect about 1536x1024. Keep the views separate with clear grey space between them; do not overlap them. All three views at the same scale, nothing cropped. The body is very long and thin, so the front and back views are small: that is correct.

SHAPE - a real large constrictor's anatomy (a boa or python) simplified into a few large masses: a thick, heavy, round body of even girth for most of its length, narrowing at the neck to a broad wedge-shaped head with heavy scales over the eyes, and tapering over its last metre to a pointed tail. The belly flat with broad pale belly scales.

THE HEAD, THE MOST IMPORTANT PART: cold and hostile, never cute. The jaws stand PARTLY OPEN at rest, the lower jaw clearly separate from the upper with a dark gap between them, showing two long curved pale fangs in front and the pale pink mouth inside. Small hard amber eyes with slit pupils under heavy brow scales. The mouth line straight and turned DOWN at its corners. IN THE FRONT VIEW TOO: the head a broad flat wedge, the parted jaws a thin DOWNWARD arch, never a smile.

PAINT - follow the SECOND reference image, a sheet of a giant ground sloth from the same game, only for its treatment and not its colours: a hand-painted texture at LOW RESOLUTION with crisp visible square texels, a few authored pixel-art marks and dark line work. The colours and pattern of the FIRST reference image: scales patterned like root bark, knotted bands of grey-brown, umber and dark brown, with small patches of green moss growing in the grooves along the back; the belly pale tan. Calm surfaces with a few strong authored details, never a busy tiled pattern, and never a patchwork of flat polygon-shaped colour blocks. Flat even lighting: paint the colours of the creature, not a light direction.
