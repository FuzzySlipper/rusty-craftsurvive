THIS DESIGN - THE DROP-SLOTH. A predatory tree sloth that lives upside down in the inverted grove, the canopy that hangs from a cave's ceiling, and drops on prey crossing the barren floor below. THE KEY FEATURE: a shaggy coat of long wine-red and maroon hair that hangs down like the grove's crowns of red filaments, the hair tips pale glowing amber, with a pale tan face and belly like the grove's root web.
