refined style nucleus is now roughly:

Aged retro-fantasy illustration translated into modular 3D game assets: old book-cover warmth, mellow ochres and sun-faded reds, softened teals/greens, painted-print surface character, bold local color, clear silhouettes, and decorative forms. The result should feel revered, timeworn, and inviting, with the emotional atmosphere of a beloved used fantasy paperback rather than a modern grimdark world.

STYLE:

Render the character as highly detailed traditional fantasy line illustration with painted color.

Let drawing and contour carry most of the form. Use precise ink-like outlines, fine internal linework, hatching, cross-hatching, and small descriptive marks to define anatomy, cloth, hair, equipment, and surface texture.

Favor elegant, slightly eccentric shapes: elongated curves, tapering forms, decorative asymmetry, organic rhythms, and distinctive silhouette design.

Color should enrich the drawing rather than replace it. Use relatively flat or translucent painted color passages over a strongly resolved underlying drawing.

Keep the linework intricate but intentional rather than noisy. The character should remain readable as a complete silhouette even when reduced in size.

Think in terms of:
- line-driven illustration
- precise ink-like contour
- fine hatching and cross-hatching
- ornamental linework
- intricate internal marks
- decorative organic curvature
- biomorphic shape language
- painted color over drawing
- crisp silhouette
- eccentric but believable forms

---

Construct the character primarily through line, contour, and unusual shape design. Keep the costume composed of a few large, clearly readable forms rather than many layered accessories. Let the silhouette be eccentric through asymmetry, tapering shapes, irregular hems, elongated curves, and unusual negative spaces.

Use fine linework and selective hatching to describe structure, folds, anatomy, and changes of plane, but do not fill surfaces with realistic microtexture. Avoid visible fabric weave, pores, leather grain, dense stitching, pervasive distressing, or highly rendered material detail.

Color should consist mainly of broad matte or translucent washes over the drawing. Preserve large quiet areas of color. Materials should read because of shape and drawing rather than realistic surface rendering.

Keep props sparse: one primary work implement and at most one secondary utility object. Avoid adventurer inventory, excessive straps, pouches, buckles, tassels, and miscellaneous equipment.

Favor high structural and illustrative detail but low material detail. The image may be intricate as a drawing without being texturally rich or visually cluttered.

STYLE:

Render the character as tight pre-digital commercial fantasy illustration with hard-edged illustrative realism.

Use precise, deliberate contours and strongly defined forms. Model the body and clothing as solid three-dimensional volumes using controlled light and shadow, smooth painted gradients, crisp highlights, and clear separation between adjacent shapes.

The image should look carefully constructed and tightly painted rather than loosely brushed or heavily outlined. Visible linework should be minimal; edges should come primarily from precise painted boundaries and value changes.

Use fairly even descriptive detail across the figure, but keep it orderly rather than busy. Materials should be recognizable through controlled painted rendering instead of modern PBR realism.

Think in terms of:
- crisp hard edges
- strong volumetric modeling
- precise painted forms
- smooth controlled gradients
- restrained brush visibility
- naturalistic anatomy
- pre-digital commercial illustration
- detailed but orderly rendering
