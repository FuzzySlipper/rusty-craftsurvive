# Paperback Sanctum generation prompts

Exact prompts used with the built-in GPT image generation tool for this approved
style-reference set. The first three images were generated independently; the
painted canyon used the original canyon as its composition reference. These are
concept illustrations, not Engine captures. Both canyon treatments and the
tundra and dungeon studies are positive references.

The owner's [source style prompt](source-style.md) is preserved verbatim. It
contains alternative treatments; the prompts below record the specific synthesis
used for each image. Prompt exclusions describe generation requests, not a reason
to reject a visible quality in the approved results. See the
[visual guide](../../visual-direction.md) for how to apply the references.

## 01-canyon-arrival

Output: [image](canyon-arrival.png)

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: exploratory environment concept for CraftSurvive, not an actual engine screenshot.
STYLE FOUNDATION: These are the images people had in their heads when they made old games, not the technology that limited them. Aged retro-fantasy illustration translated into plausible modular 3D game forms: beloved used fantasy-paperback warmth, mellow ochres and sun-faded reds, softened teal/green accents, bold local color, clear silhouettes, restrained painted-print surface character, slightly eccentric decorative shapes. Serious, atmospheric, sometimes harsh, quietly inviting; neither grimdark nor cartoonish.
Favor high structural design and low material microdetail. Tight pre-digital commercial fantasy illustration: tangible three-dimensional volumes, precise painted boundaries, controlled light and shadow, broad quiet color passages. Selective ink-like accents only where useful, no ubiquitous black outlines or dense crosshatching. Eccentric tapering curves, asymmetry and meaningful negative spaces; believable scale and proportions. Some restrained low-resolution texture character, subtly crunchy material transitions and simplified material response, with rich light and immense layered depth. Not literal pixel art, not a painterly noise filter. Stylized smooth dual-contoured landforms with purposeful cliffs and occasional angular breaks; not cubic terrain and not uniformly rounded blobs.
SETTING: Magic-dominated fantasy with common early-modern gunpowder technology, and a vastly older buried civilization. Distinguish practical recent construction from ancient unfamiliar structures. Avoid the generic medieval castle-and-village vocabulary. Ancient forms can have restrained organic geometry and eccentric construction, without stock glowing blue circuitry. Dreaminess comes from light, color, distance and atmosphere, not cute or exaggerated forms.
CONSTRAINTS: A single complete landscape image, wide composition. No panel layout, labels, captions, text, logos, UI, watermark. No anime/JRPG characters, overt toon shading, photorealistic PBR surfaces, dense accessory clutter, generic medieval towers, neon science fiction, or full-screen pixelation. Naturalistic small human figures if present. Architectural and terrain forms must be understandable as things one could explore in first person.
SCENE: The arrival at a buried site in a vast dry canyon. Wide traveler-height view from a rocky approach: huge ochre sedimentary landforms open into layered, cool distant ravines. A weather-exposed ancient structure is partly swallowed by the cliff: a tall offset oval opening, massive tapered stone ribs, small areas of dull green glazed ceramic, a dark interior. Its distinctive outline belongs to the geology; it is neither a Gothic temple nor a spaceship door. A small recent expedition shelter and modest pack cart stand on the usable ledge outside, sun-faded red canvas, practical timber frame and sparse restrained metal fittings. A lone adult traveler with one long gun slung across the back hauls one substantial strapped salvage bundle toward the cart. The burden reads as effort; no magical loot stream. Sparse dry vegetation.
COMPOSITION: Give most of the image to the canyon's immensity and the path to the site; shelter, transport, and traveler provide legible scale, not a busy foreground inventory. Clear foreground, middle distance, horizon. The route and dark entrance invite exploration.
LIGHT: Warm late-afternoon light, pale luminous sky, cool canyon shade, windborne haze in distant layers. Awe and exposure, with paperback warmth but grounded forms.
```

## 04-canyon-painted

Composition reference: [original canyon](canyon-arrival.png)

Output: [painted canyon](canyon-painted.png)

```text
Repaint this image as pre-digital fantasy paperback illustration, materially changing the rendering style while preserving its camera, canyon composition, ancient oval entrance, cart, red canvas shelter and traveler carrying a bundle.

Use the attached image as the composition reference, NOT as the desired surface rendering. The original is much too photographically detailed.

STYLE PRIORITY: bold local color, matte painted color masses, simplified material response, clear silhouettes, subtly eccentric forms. Warm ochres, sun-faded vermilion, softened teal, lavender-blue distant planes. Use precise PAINTED contours and occasional fine illustrative drawing at structural edges. Model solid volumes with broad controlled shadow shapes and smooth limited painted gradients. Let large areas of rock, cloth and architecture remain QUIET. High structural detail, LOW MATERIAL DETAIL. Omit most little pebbles, cracks and scuffs. No photographic stone texture or fabric weave. Painted-print surface character is restrained and part of the material, not a paper texture pasted over the whole image.

A serious dreamy but harsh landscape from a beloved used fantasy paperback, with shapes plausible as simple retro 3D game assets. The impossible vastness and luminous depth provide sophistication. Neither photorealistic nor cute, neither anime nor flat cartoon. Preserve believable adult human proportions. The picture must visibly read as an authored illustration at a glance rather than a modern realistic game screenshot. No thick black outlines, cel shading, pixelated overlay, watercolor blur, loose impasto, decorative border, text or UI. Single wide image.
```
