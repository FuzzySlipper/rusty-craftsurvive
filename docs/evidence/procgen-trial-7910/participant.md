# Blind participant sheet

Please play before opening trial reports, the assignment key, maps or witnesses.
The labels conceal which selection method produced a sample. They do not conceal
ordinary visible game features. This is a small calibration exercise, not a timed
performance test.

Demo: <http://192.168.1.22:37300/>

1. Open **Procgen workbench**, choose `trial-B01`, then **Load candidate** and
   **Enter**. Close the panel with **Procgen workbench**. Tab switches UI/gameplay.
2. Use WASD/mouse or the controller to explore. E / Y interacts near a station.
   Try to reach the red goal and resolve the level's obstacle. No map/witness help
   on the first attempt; if you need help, record that fact and then use it.
3. Spend up to three minutes before deciding to continue or stop. Repeat for B02,
   B03, B04, B05 and B06 in that order. It is fine not to finish a sample.
4. For each, return `Bxx: completed/not finished, help used, enjoyment 1–5,
   clarity 1–5, repetition or frustration note`. Which samples felt equivalent?
   Do not infer successful completion solely from reaching a room.

Ratings are human judgments. The system leaves enjoyment/cost metrics pending
until actual responses are retained. Automated playtester observations are
recorded separately and do not populate these ratings.
