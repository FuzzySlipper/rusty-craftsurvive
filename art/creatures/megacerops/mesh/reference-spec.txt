WHAT IT IS: a model reference sheet for building a 3D game creature: ONE a Megacerops, about 5 m long and 2.5 m at the shoulder, standing in a NEUTRAL STANCE on all four feet: legs straight down and slightly apart so every leg is clearly visible and separate, mouth closed, head level and looking straight ahead, head level, thin tail down. Exactly the same animal in three ORTHOGRAPHIC views, no perspective, flat even lighting with no cast shadows, on a plain flat light-grey background:
- TOP HALF of the sheet: the LEFT SIDE profile, head pointing to the left, the whole body from nose to tail filling the width.
- BOTTOM LEFT: the FRONT view, looking straight at its face, both forelegs and the shoulders visible.
- BOTTOM RIGHT: the BACK view, from straight behind, the tail toward the viewer and both hind legs visible.
Landscape aspect about 1536x1024. Keep the views separate with clear grey space between them; do not overlap them. All three views at the same scale.

SHAPE - follow the first reference image, the concept sheet of this animal: FAITHFUL real proportions, simplified into a few large smooth-shaded masses. a short massive body and a huge saddle-shaped head, dished in the middle, broad and long. On the very end of the nose stands a single wide Y-SHAPED BONY HORN like a giant catapult or slingshot: two blunt skin-covered prongs that spread apart from one thick base, flattened and tall. Not two horns one behind the other like a rhino's. A strong neck, and a very high hump of long spines above the shoulders giving a bison-like profile, falling away to lower hips. Short thick pillar legs, stouter than a rhino's, with four toes on the front feet and three behind, on padded feet. Small eyes well forward, small ears. A thin tail. Thick wrinkled nearly hairless hide with a stiff strip of dark bristle along the hump. Colour: warm umber-ochre with a dark band over the shoulder hump and a pale cream throat; horn prongs in the same skin, paler at the tips. Immense strength. NOT a rhinoceros, NOT a bison, NOT a hippopotamus.

PAINT - follow the second reference image, the sheet of a giant ground sloth from the same game, only for its treatment and not its colours: a hand-painted texture at LOW RESOLUTION with crisp visible square texels (about 32 texels per metre, nearest-neighbour), a few authored pixel-art marks and dark line work. Calm surfaces with a few strong authored details, never a busy tiled pattern, and never a patchwork of flat polygon-shaped colour blocks. Flat even lighting: paint the colours of the animal, not a light direction.

PROHIBITIONS: NO text, NO letters, NO labels, NO UI, NO watermark, NO frame lines. NO people, NO scale post, NO rocks, NO water, NO other objects. NOT photoreal, NOT realistic fur strands, NOT a perspective render, NOT anime, NOT cel shading or outlines.
