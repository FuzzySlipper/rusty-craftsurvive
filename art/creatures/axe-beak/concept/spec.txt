THIS DESIGN - THE AXE-BEAK. Top of the head about 4.8 m high, so the 1.8 m post reaches only to the knee. Built on the idea of a giant ground bird with a massive head: an enormous tall deep hatchet of a beak, bone-pale and hooked at the tip, nearly as long as the body is deep, carried on a thick sinewy neck that is bare of feathers. Hollow dark eyes set in the bare skull above the beak. Unlike a heavy ground bird it is gaunt and long-shanked, the body a narrow keel slung high on long legs, so the great head looks too heavy for it. Sparse ragged black plumes along the back and a tattered tail. Dust-grey body darkening to charcoal legs.
STYLE - THE MOST IMPORTANT PART. Match the second attached reference image, a sheet of a giant ground sloth from the same game, only for its treatment and not its shape or colours: a LOW-POLYGON 3D game creature with simple smooth-shaded forms from a few large masses, and a hand-painted texture at LOW RESOLUTION with crisp visible square texels (nearest-neighbour filtering), so feathers, hide, markings and eyes read as a few authored pixel-art marks with dark line work - a carefully authored retro texture on a modern-lit model, NOT a PS1 emulation, no jitter or blur. Calm surfaces with a few strong authored details, never a tiled or busy pattern, and never a patchwork of flat polygon-shaped colour blocks.

KIN TO THE GAUNT: the first attached reference image is the gaunt, the first hostile animal of the same desert. THIS IS NOT THAT ANIMAL and has none of its shape: it is a bird. Take only its mood: starved, rangy, wrong, too tall for its body, with bone-pale bare head, ash and pale-dust colouring darkening to charcoal down the legs, and ragged black bristles. It is an invented, unnerving predator of the empty desert of a fantasy frontier, not any real animal, living or extinct. It must read as dangerous from its silhouette alone, from far away, and it must read as clearly BIGGER than the gaunt.

THE KEY FEATURES: A HUGE FLIGHTLESS BIRD, RANGY AND TOO TALL. EXACTLY TWO LEGS, long, thin and stilted, each clearly separate with clear space between them, never fused. The body is lean, not fat: a narrow keeled chest, a tucked waist, bone and tendon showing. It cannot fly: any wings are small useless stumps held against the body, never spread. Big three-toed clawed feet planted wide.
