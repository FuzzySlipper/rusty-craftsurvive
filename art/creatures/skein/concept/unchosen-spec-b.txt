THIS DESIGN - THE RIVER PIGEON, from the extinct passenger pigeon: a slender, fast, pointed bird, built like a falcon-shaped dove. A wingspan of about 1 m. Long narrow pointed wings, a small neat head and a very long wedge-shaped pointed tail. Slate-blue back and head, a warm rose-copper breast fading to white under the tail, a small red eye. THE FLOCK: hundreds of birds in one enormous long winding ribbon, like a river running across the sky, thick in the middle and thinning to single birds at its ends.
STYLE - THE MOST IMPORTANT PART. Match the attached reference images, sheets of a giant ground sloth and of a soaring bird from the same game, only for their treatment and not their shape or colours: a LOW-POLYGON 3D game creature with simple smooth-shaded forms from a few large masses, and a hand-painted texture at LOW RESOLUTION with crisp visible square texels (nearest-neighbour filtering), so feathers, markings and eyes read as a few authored pixel-art marks with dark line work - a carefully authored retro texture on a modern-lit model, NOT a PS1 emulation, no jitter or blur. Faithful real bird proportions, simplified into masses. Calm surfaces with a few strong authored details, never a tiled or busy pattern, and never a patchwork of flat polygon-shaped colour blocks.

THE COUNTRY: green, wet, living country of a fantasy frontier. This bird is not a monster and not a threat, and it is never alone: the point of it is the flock, and the shape the flock makes in the sky seen from the ground. It is drawn from an extinct bird, in the way the game's other animals are.

WHAT IT IS: a model sheet from a game studio character viewer, on a plain flat warm-grey backdrop, in four panels with equal spacing, nothing overlapping or cropped: (1) top left, ONE bird as seen from directly below, wings spread; (2) top right, the same bird in strict side view in flight; (3) bottom left, the same bird in three-quarter view from above and behind, wings raised in a wing beat; (4) bottom right, a plain pale panel showing the WHOLE FLOCK as it looks from the ground far below, the birds as small dark shapes, so the flock's overall shape is obvious at a glance. Beside view 2 stands a plain dark-grey upright post exactly 1.8 m tall for scale. Landscape aspect about 1536x1024.

SOLID SHAPES ONLY: the wing feathers are a few large stiff solid shapes. No fine strands or fringes.

SETTING: a creature for a painterly early-modern frontier fantasy game whose world looks like Valheim: simple smooth-shaded forms, small textures, atmosphere carrying the mood.

PROHIBITIONS: NO text, NO letters, NO labels, NO UI, NO watermark, NO frame. NO people. NO ground, trees or scenery on the sheet. NOT photoreal, NOT realistic individual feathers, NOT anime, NOT chibi, NOT cute, NOT toon outlines or cel shading, NOT triangle-faceted polygon art.
